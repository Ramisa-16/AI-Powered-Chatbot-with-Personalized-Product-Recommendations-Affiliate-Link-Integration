# AI-Powered Chatbot with Personalized Product Recommendations & Affiliate Link Integration

## Project Overview

This project is an AI-powered chatbot designed to offer personalized conversations, product recommendations, and seamless affiliate link integration. The chatbot prioritizes products that have affiliate links and suggests relevant alternatives when needed. It retains memory and context across interactions, tailoring responses based on user preferences and past conversations. 

The chatbot provides a secure, user-friendly interface, with user authentication to protect access. The system operates on a subscription model, with both a free-tier and premium options, giving users flexibility in their usage. Users can revisit past conversations, providing a continuous, tailored experience. The platform also includes an admin dashboard for managing users, affiliate links, and tracking key analytics.

---

## AI Features

The AI component of this platform focuses on delivering personalized, efficient, and relevant product recommendations, while maintaining user privacy and improving user experience. The AI leverages custom system prompts to engage users in meaningful conversations and assist with product discovery.

### 1. **AI Chatbot**
   - **Custom System Prompts:** The AI chatbot is powered by a custom system prompt that defines how it interacts with users. The chatbot follows specific guidelines to provide relevant responses based on queries, making the conversation feel natural and tailored.
   - **Contextual Awareness:** The AI maintains context within conversations, meaning it remembers what users have said and provides responses that align with past interactions, enhancing user engagement.

### 2. **Product Recommendations**
   - **Tailored Suggestions:** The AI suggests products based on user queries and past preferences. It intelligently understands the user's needs and recommends relevant items from a catalog, ensuring that each recommendation is personalized.
   - **Affiliate Link Prioritization:** The chatbot prioritizes products with affiliate links, offering users incentives to make purchases through those links. If no affiliate links are available for a given product, the chatbot provides relevant alternatives.
   - **Real-Time Adjustments:** The AI dynamically adjusts its recommendations based on user input, ensuring that each product suggestion is aligned with the user’s changing preferences.

### 3. **Affiliate Link Integration**
   - **Seamless Affiliate Link Embedding:** Affiliate links are integrated directly into the chatbot’s responses, ensuring a smooth and non-intrusive user experience. When recommending a product, the chatbot ensures the link is embedded correctly for tracking purposes.
   - **Product Alternatives:** In cases where a product without an affiliate link is requested, the AI suggests similar alternatives that are linked to affiliate programs, maximizing revenue opportunities without compromising user experience.

### 4. **Memory & Context Retention**
   - **Personalized Conversations:** The AI remembers user preferences, past interactions, and frequently discussed products. This allows the system to tailor its responses to each individual user, making the conversation feel more personalized over time.
   - **Contextual Understanding:** By retaining context across multiple sessions, the AI can provide continuity and relevance, improving the accuracy of product recommendations and making interactions feel more fluid.

### 5. **User Authentication**
   - **Secure Login:** Users are required to authenticate their identity to access the platform. This ensures that sensitive user data is protected, and the system can personalize recommendations more effectively based on the user's profile.
   - **Custom Profiles:** Each user has a personalized profile, and the AI can adapt its suggestions based on past conversations and preferences.

### 6. **Subscription Model**
   - **Free-tier Access:** The platform offers a free-tier subscription with limited daily usage, allowing users to experience the chatbot’s capabilities while keeping access controlled.
   - **Premium Access:** Premium users gain full access to the chatbot's features, including unlimited product recommendations and more personalized interaction history.

### 7. **Conversation History**
   - **Continuity Across Sessions:** Users can revisit their past conversations with the AI, ensuring a continuous experience without having to re-explain their needs or preferences.
   - **Personalized Interactions:** By reviewing conversation history, the AI can adapt and fine-tune its recommendations based on how the user interacts with the system over time.
